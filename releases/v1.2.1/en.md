Faster search filters, an assistant that fits the screen and tidier settings

### Fixes
- **settings**: an index on the left and one card per section; the password form shows the current password first, the new one and its repeat side by side, rules that tick as you type and a "Show passwords" toggle
- **assistant**: the page fits the screen without scrolling, with a compact header, the chat scrolling inside and suggestions always at hand on the right

### Improvements
- **search**: filters open instantly: the browser keeps the last list and the server loads it at startup and refreshes it in the background
