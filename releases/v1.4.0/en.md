A real phone layout, and an Android app that updates itself

Android app 1.1.0: https://forgejo.home-sousa.me/Tiago/gameTrackerVault/raw/tag/v1.4.0/apps/frontend/public/downloads/gametracker-vault.apk (or in the app, under Help → Android app). If you have app 1.0.0, install this one by hand once; from then on the app updates itself.

### Features
- **mobile**: on phones, a fixed bottom navigation (Home, Search, Stats, Assistant, Settings) and a compact top bar; Admin moves to the avatar menu
- **mobile**: game pages show the cover next to the title, and "My shelf" becomes a bar that opens the panel as a sheet from the bottom
- **mobile**: the assistant fills the screen with suggestions inside the chat; smaller titles, cards and controls everywhere, and equal-width theme and language buttons
- **android**: the app checks your server for a newer version when it opens and offers to update; it downloads and installs inside the app after one confirmation, keeping your session and server
- **android**: Help shows the installed and latest app versions, with "Update app" and "Check for updates"
