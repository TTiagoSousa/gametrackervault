Android app: GameTracker Vault on your phone, connected to your server

Download the APK: https://forgejo.home-sousa.me/Tiago/gameTrackerVault/raw/tag/v1.3.0/apps/frontend/public/downloads/gametracker-vault.apk (or in the app, under Help → Android app)

### Features
- **android**: an Android app (Android 8+) that opens GameTracker Vault full screen with its own icon; on first launch you type your server's address, and after that it opens straight away and always matches your server's version
- **android**: links to other sites open in the browser, the Back button goes back, and a "can't reach the server" screen offers to try again or change server
- **help**: an "Android app" section with the download (version and size) and install steps; "Android app" in the account menu, and "Change server" there inside the app

### Improvements
- **auth**: a brief network drop no longer sends you to the login page
